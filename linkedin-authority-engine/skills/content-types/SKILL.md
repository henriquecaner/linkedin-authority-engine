# 16 content types

## Overview

Pick the type based on your objective and what you have to share.

---

## 1. Truth and Hope

**When to use:** Share a hard truth + offer a way forward

**Structure:**

→ Hard/difficult truth

→ Why it matters

→ But there's hope

→ Here's the path

---

## 2. Old Way, New Way

**When to use:** Introduce a new approach to an existing topic

**Structure:**

→ How it used to be

→ Problems with the old way

→ The new way

→ Why it works

---

## 3. I Learned Something This Week

**When to use:** Share a recent insight from personal experience

**Structure:**

→ What I learned

→ Context

→ Why it mattered

→ How to apply it

---

## 4. Statistics Post

**When to use:** Open with a surprising statistic

**Structure:**

→ Shocking statistic

→ Why it happens

→ What it means

→ How to benefit from it

---

## 5. The Magic of X

**When to use:** Highlight the benefits of a specific topic

**Structure:**

→ What "X" is

→ Why it's magic

→ Specific benefits

→ How to get started

---

## 6. Before-and-After

**When to use:** Share a transformation story

**Structure:**

→ The before situation

→ What changed

→ The after situation

→ Lesson learned

---

## 7. Round-up List

**When to use:** Gather resources on a specific topic

**Structure:**

→ Topic intro

→ List of resources

→ Why each one is valuable

→ Invitation to contribute

---

## 8. Behind the Scenes

**When to use:** Show the behind-the-scenes of a process

**Structure:**

→ What you're building

→ Process/challenges

→ Takeaways

→ Next steps

---

## 9. Tell a Story

**When to use:** Connect emotionally

**Structure:**

→ Character

→ Situation/conflict

→ Action taken

→ Outcome

→ Lesson

---

## 10. How-to (Tutorial)

**When to use:** Teach a process

**Structure:**

→ Clear title "How to do X"

→ Numbered step-by-step

→ Specific tips

→ Expected outcome

---

## 11. Contrarian Opinion

**When to use:** Grab attention with a unique view

**Structure:**

→ Clear contrarian opinion

→ Why you believe it

→ Data/experience that backs it

→ Opening for debate

---

## 12. Lessons from Mentor

**When to use:** Talk about lessons from a mentor

**Structure:**

→ Who the mentor is

→ Context of the lesson

→ Specific lessons

→ How you applied them

---

## 13. Mistakes and Failures

**When to use:** Share vulnerability

**Structure:**

→ The mistake/failure

→ How it happened

→ Consequences

→ What you learned

---

## 14. Create a Villain

**When to use:** Build contrast to land a message

**Structure:**

→ Introduce the "villain" (a mindset or idea)

→ Why it's a problem

→ How it hurts you

→ The alternative/solution

---

## 15. Framework/System

**When to use:** Present a structured method

**Structure:**

→ Framework name

→ Context/problem it solves

→ Numbered steps

→ Expected outcome

---

## 16. Data-Driven

**When to use:** Present data-based insights

**Structure:**

→ Headline data point

→ Context of the analysis

→ Derived insights

→ Practical application

---

## Selection guide

| Objective | Recommended types |
| --- | --- |
| Educate | How-to, Framework, Statistics |
| Inspire | Story, Before-After, Mentor |
| Engage | Contrarian, Villain, I Learned |
| Convert | Before-After, Magic of X |
| Authority | Data-Driven, Framework |
