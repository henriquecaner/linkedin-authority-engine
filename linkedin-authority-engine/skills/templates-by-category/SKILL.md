# Templates by category

> Ready-to-use templates for each post type. Personalize with your own data.

## 1. Career lesson

**Recommended structure:** Storytelling or BAB
**Recommended hooks:** Story, Authority Proof

### Template

```
[HOOK - Defining moment]
The day {defining event} changed how I see {topic}.

[CONTEXT - Story setup]
It was {year/period}.

I was {initial situation}.

I thought {prior belief}.

[DEVELOPMENT - The turning point]
Then {event happened}.

{Specific detail of the moment}.

{Initial reaction}.

{What I realized}.

[LESSON - The takeaway]
What I learned:

{Lesson 1 - in one sentence}.

{Lesson 2 - in one sentence}.

{Lesson 3 - in one sentence}.

[CONCLUSION]
Today, {X} years later, {how this changed your career}.

{Final reflection}.

[CTA]
{CTA aligned with the objective}
```

### Filled-in example

```
The day I lost my biggest client changed how I see B2B sales.

It was 2019.

I was at my peak.

$500k in recurring revenue. A team of 12. I thought I knew everything.

Then the phone rang.

"We've decided not to renew."

4 words. 40% of revenue. Gone.

I didn't sleep for 2 weeks.

I reviewed everything. I found the problem.

What I learned:

1. Revenue concentration is a time bomb.

2. A relationship without delivered value is an illusion.

3. The client who never complains is the one who walks away.

Today, 5 years later, no single client makes up more than 15% of revenue.

That call hurt. But it saved me.

If you have one client who accounts for more than 30% of your revenue, this is your sign.

Follow me for more B2B sales lessons (the kind that hurt).
```

## 2. Achievement

**Recommended structure:** BAB or FAB
**Recommended hooks:** Proof of Work, Transformation

### Template

```
[HOOK - Specific result]
From {number_before} to {number_after} in {period}.

Without {something people assume is necessary}.

[CONTEXT - The before]
{Period} ago, I was {initial situation}.

{Specific bad metric}.

{What wasn't working}.

[DEVELOPMENT - What changed]
The turning point came when {specific change}.

I made {number} changes:

1. {Change 1} → {result}

2. {Change 2} → {result}

3. {Change 3} → {result}

[PROOF - Detailed numbers]
The numbers:

• Before: {metric}
• After: {metric}
• Time: {period}

[CONCLUSION - The big lesson]
The difference wasn't {what people think}.

It was {the real insight}.

[CTA]
{CTA aligned with the objective}
```

### Filled-in example

```
From 3 to 47 leads/month in 90 days.

No ads. No marketing team. No cold calls.

3 months ago, I was stuck.

3 leads a month. 2 were unqualified. A 6-month sales cycle.

Something was badly wrong.

The turning point came when I stopped selling and started teaching.

I made 3 changes:

1. 3 posts/week teaching what I charge to do → Authority

2. A lead magnet with a real framework (not a generic PDF) → Qualification

3. A specific CTA instead of "let's talk" → Clarity

The numbers:

• Before: 3 leads/month
• After: 47 leads/month
• Time: 90 days
• Cost: $0 in media

The difference wasn't more content.

It was content that shows what I do, not that talks about it.

```

## Usage tips

1. **Personalize the numbers** - Replace every placeholder with real data
2. **Keep paragraphs short** - 3 lines max each
3. **Count the characters** - Stay between 1,250-2,500
4. **Pick 1 CTA** - Don't mix several
5. **Validate with the scoring system** - Target: ≥9/10
