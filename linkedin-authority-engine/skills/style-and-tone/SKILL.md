# Style and tone guide

## Ideal tone of voice

### DO:

✅ Be direct and practical (not theoretical)

✅ Use conversational language ("write the way you talk")

✅ Be specific with numbers and data

✅ Tell real stories (emotion + data)

✅ Admit mistakes and failures (vulnerability sells)

### DON'T:

❌ Be vague or use unnecessary jargon

❌ Use dense blocks of text (−71% reach)

❌ Promise without delivering

❌ Corporate language

❌ Complex words (average >5 letters = −39%)

---

## 360Brew 2026 specs

| Parameter | Optimal value |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 1,250-2,500 characters |
| Paragraphs | At least 14 short ones |
| Words per paragraph | Up to 19 |
| Average letters per word | ≤5 |
| Reading level | 5th to 7th grade |

---

## Formatting that works

✅ Generous white space

✅ Short paragraphs (2-3 lines)

✅ Lists when appropriate (→ or numbered)

✅ Emojis in moderation (visual guides)

✅ Arrows (→) to list points

---

## Hooks that perform in 2026

### ✅ USE:

**Authority hook:**

"After analyzing 2.4M posts..."

**Narrative hook:**

"I got laid off in 2023. Here's what I learned:"

**Tension hook:**

"95% of creators get this wrong:"

**Proof-of-work hook:**

"I spent $50k testing this. The result:"

### ❌ AVOID:

❌ "What do you think?" (generic, punished)

❌ "Good morning, LinkedIn!" (zero value)

❌ "Agree?" (lazy)

---

## Good vs bad examples

### BAD:

"Lots of clients" → How many?

"Recently" → When?

"Incredible results" → Which numbers?

### GOOD:

"47 clients in 6 months"

"In March 2025"

"+300% engagement in 90 days"

---

## Authenticity principles

1. **Specific numbers** (not "a lot" or "several")
2. **Real names** or concrete situations
3. **Conversational tone** (as if talking to a friend)
4. **Zero unnecessary** jargon
5. **Vulnerability** present (mistakes, failures)
