# Post-publication protocol — the critical 90 minutes

> Deliver at the end of any mode (Guided, Rewrite, Thread) together with the final post.

## Why this window exists

360Brew tests each post with 2-5% of the most active followers during the first 90 minutes. The result of that test determines whether the post circulates to larger audiences. A good post without a post-publication protocol performs worse than an average post with a well-executed protocol.

## Signals measured in this period

| Signal | Weight | How to optimize |
|-------|------|---------------|
| Saves | Maximum (5x a like) | Reference content + save CTA |
| Dwell time >15 sec | High (unlocks distribution) | Progressive structure, carousel |
| Long comments (3+ sentences) | High (2x a like) | Reply with depth, not "thanks!" |
| Likes | Low (residual) | Don't focus here |
| Dwell time <3 sec | Negative (disqualifies) | Strong hook in the very first line |

## Immediate protocol after publishing

### First 5 minutes

1. Publish the post
2. Immediately add the **first comment** with:
   - Link (if you have one) + context that didn't fit in the post
   - A question that invites substantial replies

### First 90 minutes

3. Reply to **every comment** with 3+ sentences (not one-word replies)
4. Don't leave the platform. The algorithm tracks the author's activity as a signal of an active community
5. Like and comment on posts from other accounts that have historically engaged with you (reciprocity signal)

## What to avoid

- **"Post and ghost"** (publish and disappear). Suppresses future distribution.
- Replying with emojis or "Thanks!". Doesn't count as a long comment.
- Asking for likes. A residual signal that doesn't move distribution.
- Editing the post in the first 30 minutes. Restarts the algorithm's evaluation.

## Recommended timing by objective

| Objective | Day | Window (BRT) |
|----------|-----|--------------|
| Authority | Tuesday | 08:00-10:00 |
| Sales | Thursday | 11:00-13:00 |
| Engagement | Sunday | 10:00-12:00 |

> **Note:** With the 90-minute test window, timing still matters, but what matters more is being present and replying to comments within that period. A post published at the right time + "post and ghost" performs worse than one published off-peak with active engagement.

## Close the loop

After the 90 minutes (and again at 7 days), record the real performance in Unabyss to calibrate topics, hooks, and CTAs with data from your own profile rather than generic benchmarks.

## Deliverable summary

Compact version to paste at the end of any mode:

```
POST-PUBLICATION PROTOCOL

1. Publish
2. First comment immediately: link + substantial question
3. Next 90 min: reply to every comment with 3+ sentences
4. Don't edit the post in the first 30 min
5. Don't leave the platform — interact on other accounts' posts
```
