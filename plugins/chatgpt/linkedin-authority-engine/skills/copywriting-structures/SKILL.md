# Copywriting structures

## AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)

**Best when:** You want strong conversion

**1. Attention** - Strong hook

**2. Interest** - Tell a story

**3. Desire** - Show the benefits

**4. Action** - Clear CTA

---

## PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution)

**Best when:** Solving a problem

**1. Problem** - Identify the pain

**2. Agitation** - Intensify it, show the consequences

**3. Solution** - Present the path forward

---

## BAB (Before, After, Bridge)

**Best when:** You have a tangible result

**1. Before** - The situation before (negative)

**2. After** - The situation after (positive)

**3. Bridge** - What changed (the path)

---

## FAB (Features, Advantages, Benefits)

**Best when:** You have a method or system

**1. Features** - What it IS

**2. Advantages** - How it WORKS

**3. Benefits** - What you GAIN

---

## Star-Story-Solution

**Best when:** You have a success case

**1. Star** - Introduce the protagonist

**2. Story** - Tell the journey

**3. Solution** - Reveal the method or lesson

---

## APP (Agree, Promise, Preview)

**Best when:** You want to engage from the start

**1. Agree** - Establish common ground

**2. Promise** - Make a specific promise

**3. Preview** - Give a taste of the content

---

## HSO (Hook, Story, Offer)

**Best when:** A personal narrative that ends in an offer

**1. Hook** - A line that stops the scroll (proof of work or tension)

**2. Story** - A story with a beginning, conflict, and turn

**3. Offer** - An offer or CTA that follows naturally from the story

---

## Storytelling (Setup, Conflict, Resolution, Lesson)

**Best when:** Emotional connection is the goal

**1. Setup** - Context and character (you or a client)

**2. Conflict** - The problem, the crisis, what went wrong

**3. Resolution** - How it was solved (with specific details)

**4. Lesson** - What the reader takes away

---

## Choosing guide

| Situation | Structure |
| --- | --- |
| You have a result | BAB |
| Solving a problem | PAS |
| You want conversion | AIDA |
| You have a method | FAB |
| You have a personal story | Star-Story-Solution |
| You want fast engagement | APP |
| Narrative + offer | HSO |
| Emotional connection | Storytelling |
